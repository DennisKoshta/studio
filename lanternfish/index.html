The machine

          
16 registers (r15 = sp), 64 KiB, 4 MHz, 160×120 in 16 colours, a blitter, and four voices. Assembly files are .lfa, and the C-like Glint language is .glint.
             The full references are docs/SPEC.md, docs/ASSEMBLY.md and docs/GLINT.md in the repository.

          
Tiny example


start:  li r1, BLT_COLOR
        li r0, 9          ; amber
        st [r1], r0
        li r1, BLT_CMD
        li r0, BLT_CLEAR
        st [r1], r0
.hold:  wait
        jmp .hold
